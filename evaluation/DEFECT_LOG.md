# Frozen V1 defect log

The V1 brief was reliable enough to recover the project, but not sufficient for
trading research decisions.

1. Executive bullets were unranked summaries rather than a decision hierarchy.
2. Facts, source forecasts, source opinions and Macro Sage inference shared the
   same prose shape.
3. Asset views contained direction, drivers and risks but no causal
   transmission, catalyst, invalidation or explicit countercase contract.
4. Model-proposed confidence had no deterministic evidence rubric.
5. The report had no scenario or disagreement map.
6. The report did not permit or foreground an explicit “no supported setup”
   outcome.
7. It lacked a hard actionability gate for missing market prices, positioning
   and calendar data.
8. Source citations were valid but attached at section level rather than to
   each material claim.
9. Model names and token counts consumed first-page attention better used for
   changes, regime and decision risks.
10. The hosted PDF was discoverable only through a short-retention workflow
    artifact and had no configured delivery channel.

Already-fixed reliability defects remain regression cases rather than V2
design goals: opaque citation hashes, delayed-run date drift, normal no-data
failure, unsafe artifact bodies, and evictable-only history.

# Decision-brief evaluation rubric

Score each dimension from 1 (unacceptable) to 5 (excellent). A critical defect
is an automatic failure regardless of the average score.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Factual faithfulness | Material invention or distortion | Mostly faithful with minor ambiguity | Every audited claim matches its cited evidence |
| Citation validity | Missing or unknown citations | Valid links with some imprecision | Every material claim has precise supporting citations |
| Fact versus inference | Categories are blurred | Usually distinguishable | Facts, source views, disagreement and inference are explicit |
| Prioritization | Generic recap | Major items present | Ranked developments make the decision relevance obvious |
| Disagreement | Forced consensus | Disagreement mentioned | Each side and the resolving evidence are represented fairly |
| Transmission | Unsupported labels | Plausible but incomplete causality | Clear causal path across the relevant assets |
| Catalysts and invalidation | Absent or fabricated | Present but generic | Sourced catalyst, observable trigger and change-of-mind condition |
| Scenario differentiation | Cosmetic variants | Distinct narratives | Distinct signposts and cross-asset consequences |
| Unsupported precision | Invented prices, dates or probabilities | Minor overprecision | No precision beyond cited or calculated inputs |
| Daily readability | Cannot be scanned | Usable with effort | First page works in roughly two minutes |
| Temporal accuracy | Stale material called new | Mostly correct | New, changed, carried and retired states are correct |
| Actionability | Unqualified trade call | Conditional research idea | Thesis, expression, why-now, risks, countercase and data gate are clear |

Critical deterministic gates:

- the V2 schema validates;
- every nested citation resolves to one manifest document;
- the code-derived source register equals the nested citation set;
- failed-source counts agree with the acquisition manifest;
- no unsupported current-price, positioning or market-confirmation language;
- each candidate expression contains catalyst, invalidation and countercase;
- `ready_for_review` is impossible without verified market data;
- normalized themes and expressions are not duplicated;
- all six regime dimensions and all three scenario kinds are present.

Promotion gates:

- zero critical unsupported facts or numbers in at least 100 audited claims;
- at least 95% substantive citation support;
- 100% citation resolution;
- V2 preferred for decision usefulness on at least 70% of ten or more blinded,
  same-corpus comparisons;
- no grounding regression against V1;
- ten consecutive scheduled reports or intentional no-data outcomes after
  promotion.
